The ones everyone asks.

Will customers know it's automated?

It reads like a normal text because you wrote it. The point isn't to fool anyone. It's that a real reply arrives in seconds instead of hours.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. It runs on the number that's already on your ute, your signage and every business card you've handed out.

What if I don't use a CRM?

Plenty of people don't. It can run off a spreadsheet and your phone. If a CRM would genuinely help I'll say so, but I'm not going to sell you software you'd never open.

What does it cost?

Each build is quoted separately, with a discount if you take several at once. There's a small ongoing cost for the messaging itself, which I pass on at cost. You'll get the number on the call.

Is texting customers allowed?

Replying to someone who just rang you is fine. Bulk marketing texts are a different thing with real rules attached, and I build these to stay on the right side of that line.

Find the leak first.

Fifteen minutes to work out where enquiries are actually going missing. Sometimes it's one automation. Sometimes it's nothing, and I'll tell you that.
