What I don't do.

I don't quote your jobs or promise customers anything on your behalf. Messages only say what you've approved.

Anything about price, the work itself or a complaint comes straight to you. The system handles the chasing, you handle the trade.

Questions people ask.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. It runs on the number that's already on your ute, your signage and every business card you've handed out.

I use job management software. Does it work with that?

If your software can connect to other tools, we'll hook into it. If it can't, it can run alongside it. I'll check yours on the first call.

Will customers know it's automated?

It reads like a normal text because you wrote it. The point isn't to fool anyone. It's that they hear back in seconds instead of hours.

What does it cost?

Each build is quoted separately, with a discount if you take a few. There's a small ongoing cost for the messaging, passed on at cost. You'll get the number on the call.
