The engagement

Four phases, in order.

Each one stands on its own. You can stop after the assessment and take the plan elsewhere — I'd rather that than build something you're not sure about.

PHASE 01

Opportunity assessment

I sit with how the business actually runs, not how the org chart says it does. Where the repetitive manual work is, where revenue leaks out, which of it AI or automation could genuinely take over — and I rank those by what they'd return against what they'd cost. Some things on that list will be "don't bother", and I'll say so.

PHASE 02

The strategy

What should be automated, what should use AI and what shouldn't, which systems need to connect to which, what gets built first, what it costs and what it should give back. One document you could hand to someone else and they'd know exactly what to do.

PHASE 03

Implementation

The workflows get built. CRM, email, SMS, calendar and whatever else you're running get connected. AI goes in where it earns its place. Everything gets tested against real cases before it touches a customer, then it goes live.

PHASE 04

Optimisation

Systems drift. Things break quietly. Ongoing means I'm monitoring what's running, fixing what stops, improving what's clunky, and looking for the next thing worth doing now that the first one's paid for itself.

Questions

Fair things to ask.

Is this just ChatGPT with extra steps?

Some of it is a language model doing a job it's good at. Plenty of it is plumbing between systems with no AI in it at all. The point is the outcome, not the technology — if a simple automation beats an AI one, you get the simple one.

Are you going to tell me to replace my staff?

No. Almost always the win is taking the tedious half of a job away so the person can do the half that actually needs them. That's an easier sell internally too, which matters if you want it adopted.

What if the assessment says do nothing?

Then it says that, and you've spent a small amount to avoid spending a large one. I'd rather tell you that than build something you didn't need.

Where does our data go?

We agree that before anything is built — what's allowed to leave your systems, what isn't, and which tools are acceptable. If there are rules in your industry about it, they shape the design from the start rather than getting patched in later.

What does it cost?

The assessment is a fixed one-off, scoped to the size of the business. Implementation is quoted per build once we know what we're building, and ongoing support is monthly if you want it. You'll get real numbers on the call — quoting before I've seen the business would be a guess.

Do I have to be an existing client?

No. This runs on its own for businesses that don't need anything to do with Google.
