Before you book.

What does it cost?

Automations are quoted per build, depending on how many you want and what they need to connect to. Custom systems are scoped and priced before anything starts. Consulting begins with a fixed-price assessment.

I'll give you a real number on the call once I've seen the business. Quoting before that would be a guess, and you'd be right not to trust it.

Do I have to change the software I use?

Usually not. Most of what I build connects to what you've already got: your phone number, inbox, calendar, spreadsheet or CRM. If something genuinely needs replacing, I'll tell you why before anything changes.

How long does it take?

A single automation is usually running inside a fortnight. Bigger systems get a timeline in the plan, so you know before you agree to anything.

Will my customers know it's automated?

It reads like a normal message because you approve the wording. The point isn't to fool anyone. It's that a real reply arrives in seconds instead of hours.

Am I locked in?

No. Builds are quoted as one-off jobs, and ongoing support is month to month if you want it.

What happens to my data?

We agree on that before anything is built: what's stored where, what AI is allowed to see, and what never leaves your systems.

I'm flat out already. Do I need this?

Being flat out is usually why the admin is slipping. But I'm not going to talk you into anything. If there's nothing worth building, I'll tell you that on the call.
