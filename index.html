Questions

Before you book.

How long until I see results?

Six to twelve weeks for rankings to move properly. You'll see the first grid at the end of month one so you know it's happening, but I wouldn't judge it before month three — that's why the minimum term exists.

What does it cost?

Local SEO is a flat monthly fee with a three-month minimum, then it rolls on. Automations are quoted per build depending on how many you want and what they need to connect to. Consulting is scoped after the assessment.

I'll give you a real number on the call once I've seen the business — quoting before that would be a guess, and you'd be right not to trust it.

Can you get me to number one?

No, and anyone who promises that is guessing. I don't control Google. What I can tell you is exactly what I'll do, and you'll see the grid every month — so you'll know within eight weeks whether it's working.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not to start. Most of the calls come from the Maps listing, and that can do most of a website's job if it's built properly. If a site would help after that, it's quoted separately — never bundled into the monthly.

What if I already have someone doing my SEO?

Then take the free audit anyway and use it to check their work. If they're doing it well you'll see that in fifteen minutes, and you should keep them.

Am I locked in?

Three months, because GBP changes genuinely take six to twelve weeks to show up and a client who leaves at week six never finds out whether it worked. After that it's month to month.

I'm flat out already — do I need this?

Probably not right now, and I'm not going to talk you into it. Worth knowing the listing's there when things go quiet, because they always do eventually.

What happens on the call?

I ask what's broken and how you currently handle it, then show you what I found. If there's a fit I'll tell you what I'd do and what it costs. If there isn't, I'll say so — you still keep the audit.
