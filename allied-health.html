    The systems.

    Most clinics have some of this already. We fill the gaps, not replace what works.

    Reminders that get repliesConfirm or reschedule by text

    Reminders go out at the right time, and patients can confirm or move their booking by replying. You find out about a cancellation while there's still time to fill it.

    Cancellation fillingThe waitlist gets offered first

    A slot opens, the waitlist is offered it, first to reply gets it. Nobody at the desk has to ring around.

    New patient intakeForms back before they arrive

    Intake forms go out when they book, come back before the appointment, and get filed in the right place.

    RecallsWho's dropped off, flagged

    Patients who haven't been back in a set time get flagged, and a friendly check-in goes out. Your team decides who gets a call.

    After-hours enquiriesThe common questions, answered

    Hours, prices, parking and referrals get answered straight away. Anything clinical is passed to a practitioner, never answered by the system.

    What I don't do.

    No clinical advice, ever. The system handles bookings, reminders and admin. Anything about symptoms, treatment or results goes to a practitioner.

    Patient information is handled carefully. What's stored where, and what AI can and can't see, is agreed before anything is built, and your practice software stays the source of truth.

    Questions people ask.

    Our practice software already sends reminders. Is this still useful?

    Maybe not for reminders, and I'll say so. The gaps are usually cancellation filling, recalls and after-hours enquiries. We use what you have and build around it.

    What about patient privacy?

    Health information gets extra care. We agree up front what the system can access, where anything is stored, and what never leaves your practice software.

    Will it ever give health advice?

    No. Anything clinical is passed to a practitioner. The system only handles admin.

    What does it cost?

    Each build is quoted separately once I've seen how your clinic runs. You'll get a real number on the call.
