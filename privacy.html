We collect information you give us directly: your name, business name, email address, phone number and any details you share when you enquire, book a call or become a client. We use third-party tools to run the business, and your information may be stored in them. These include Google (Workspace, Google Business Profile, Google Cloud), scheduling and email software, and automation software. Each has its own privacy policy.

Google user data

Where we connect to Google services on your behalf or our own, we use Google OAuth. We request only the access needed to perform the agreed work, we do not transfer that data to others, and we do not use it for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Storage and security

Information is stored in the third-party services listed above, protected by account credentials and two-factor authentication where available. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email jax@emanateai.com.au and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Marketing and opt-out

If we contact you about our services, every message identifies us and includes a way to opt out. Ask us to stop and we will.
