    What I don't do.

    I don't value properties, I don't negotiate, and I don't take a cut of any sale. The appraisal, the price and the selling are yours.

    I'm paid for building and running the system, not for the sale. That keeps it simple for both of us.

    Questions people ask.

    Is this spam?

    No. Messages only go to people who already have a relationship with your agency, they're sent in your name, and every one includes an easy way to opt out. Opt-outs are removed straight away.

    Which CRM do I need?

    If your CRM or property management software can export contacts, we can work with it. I'll check what yours can do on the first call.

    Who do the messages come from?

    Your agency. Your name, your number or email, your voice. The system sits in the background.

    What does it cost?

    The reactivation campaign is a fixed price based on the size of your database. Ongoing systems are a monthly fee with no lock-in. You'll get real numbers on the call.

    What if nobody replies?

    Then you've found out your database is cold for a fixed fee, and I'll tell you what I think the reason is.
