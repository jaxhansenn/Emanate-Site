How a campaign runs.

Most of the work is mine. Yours is approving the messages and turning up to appraisals.

1

A quick look at your database

How many contacts, how old, and what your CRM can export. If it's too thin to be worth it, I'll tell you before you spend anything.

2

Messages written with you

They go out in your agency's name and sound like you. You approve every word, and every message includes an easy opt-out.

3

The campaign runs

Replies are sorted as they come in. Ready-to-sell people are booked into your calendar, not-yet people go into follow-ups, and opt-outs are removed.

4

You get the report

Who replied, who booked, and who's worth a personal call. Then you decide whether to keep the appraisal catcher running.

What I don't do.

I don't value properties, I don't negotiate, and I don't take a cut of any sale. The appraisal, the price and the selling are yours.

I'm paid for building and running the system, not for the sale. That keeps it simple for both of us.

Questions agents ask.

Is this spam?

No. Messages only go to people who already have a relationship with your agency, they're sent in your name, and every one includes an easy way to opt out. Anyone who opts out is removed straight away.

Which CRM do I need?

If your CRM can export your contacts, we can work with it. I'll check what yours can do on the first call.

Who do the messages come from?

Your agency. Your name, your number or email, your voice. The system sits in the background.

What does it cost?

The campaign is a fixed price, quoted upfront based on the size of your database. The appraisal catcher is a monthly fee with no lock-in. You'll get real numbers on the call.

What if nobody replies?

Then you've found out your database is cold for a fixed fee, and I'll tell you what I think the reason is. I won't pretend otherwise.
