The problem

It's almost never the thing people think it is.

Most owners assume they need a new website or more ads. Nine times out of ten the listing itself is the problem — wrong primary category, no service area set, a description written in 2019, four photos, and reviews nobody ever asked for.

Google reads all of that as a business that isn't really operating. Then it hands the top three spots to someone who filled the form in properly.

SYMPTOM

You search and see yourself

From your phone, on your own wifi, you're number one. That's Google recognising you — not what a stranger two suburbs over sees.

SYMPTOM

Good reviews, no calls

Five stars and nothing coming in usually means visibility, not reputation. Nobody's rejecting you. They're not seeing you.

SYMPTOM

Busy season carries you

Referrals fill the diary until they don't. The listing is what catches work in the quiet months, and it takes weeks to warm up.

Month one

The build.

The unglamorous work that actually moves rankings. Done once, properly, before anything else starts.

  • Claim and verify the listing — or recover one that's been compromised
  • Primary and secondary category audit — the highest-impact fix, and the one nobody self-diagnoses
  • Services built out properly, with real descriptions
  • Business description written for search, not for you
  • Hours, service area, attributes, booking and contact links
  • Photo audit and first upload batch, named correctly
  • Q&A seeded with the questions customers actually ask
  • Name, address, phone consistency checked across the main directories
  • Review request system — request link, SMS template, QR card for the ute or the front desk
  • Baseline captured — insights and a ranking grid, before I touch anything
Every month after

The maintenance.

Google treats a stale listing as a closed one. This is the part that keeps it moving.

  • Two to four Google Posts
  • New photos added and named
  • Review monitoring, response drafting, request nudges
  • Ranking grid re-scanned on the same keyword, so it's comparable
  • A plain monthly report — grid before and after, position, calls, direction requests, website clicks, reviews gained, what I did, what's next
Straight answers

What I won't tell you.

That I can guarantee position one

I don't control Google and neither does anyone selling you a guarantee. You'll get the same grid I'm looking at, every month, so you can judge it yourself.

That it's a quick fix

Rankings take six to twelve weeks to move properly. Anyone promising next week is either lying or about to do something that gets your listing suspended.

That you definitely need a website

Often you don't, at least not first. The listing does most of the job. If a site helps later it's a separate quote, never folded into the monthly.

What's not included

Social media management, paid ads, email marketing, logo and branding, and copywriting outside the listing. If you need those I'll tell you who does them well.
